# Nimbus N7 — Site Safety & Operating Rules

These rules apply to every site that operates Nimbus N7 drones. Site managers
must confirm them during installation and review them once a year.

## Before flight
- Every flight area needs a valid geofence file (`zone.cfg`) on the drone's SD
  card. Without it, the drone refuses to arm.
- Aisles must be at least 2.4 m wide for autonomous scanning.
- Fire exits and first-aid stations must be marked as no-fly zones in the
  geofence.

## People and drones
- Drones keep a minimum clearance of 0.8 m from detected people (Cirrus 5.0+).
- Staff working in an active scan zone must wear the high-visibility vest
  issued with the site kit; the vest's reflective strips help the obstacle
  sensors detect people.
- Manual override may only be used by staff who completed the Nimbus Pilot
  course. Crashes during manual override are not covered by the warranty.

## Charging and docks
- Docks must be mounted on a non-flammable wall, at least 1 m from shelving
  that holds packaging material.
- A dock serves one drone. Swapping drones between docks is allowed, but each
  dock must be re-paired in the Halo portal afterwards.
- Damaged or swollen battery packs must be removed from service immediately and
  returned through the Tallinn parts office for recycling.

## Incidents
- Any collision, fall, or near-miss must be reported in the Halo portal within
  24 hours.
- After a collision, the drone stays grounded until a site manager completes a
  visual inspection checklist.
- Repeated incidents (three within 30 days) trigger a free on-site safety
  review by Nimbus field engineers.
