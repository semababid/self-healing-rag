# Nimbus Robotics: IT & Security Policy

## Accounts and sign-in
- Every employee signs in to company systems, including the Halo portal, with
  single sign-on (SSO).
- Multi-factor authentication is mandatory; hardware security keys are issued
  to everyone with admin rights to the drone fleet.
- Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Forced periodic rotation is not
  used; passwords are changed only after a suspected compromise.

## Devices
- Engineers receive a laptop with 32 GB of RAM; other staff receive 16 GB.
- Laptops are replaced every 4 years, or sooner if they fail.
- Personal devices may access email and chat, but not the drone fleet console.

## Network and remote access
- Remote access to internal systems requires the company VPN.
- The drone fleet runs on a separate warehouse network; drones never connect
  to the office Wi-Fi.
- Guest Wi-Fi is available in all three offices and is isolated from the
  internal network.

## Data handling
- Warehouse inventory data belongs to the customer and is stored in the EU
  (Frankfurt region).
- Scan data is retained for 18 months, then deleted automatically, unless the
  customer contract specifies a different period.
- Customer data may not be copied to personal devices or shared outside
  approved tools.

## Reporting incidents
- Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@nimbus.example
  within 1 hour of discovery.
- Lost or stolen laptops are wiped remotely; report them immediately.
