# Nimbus N5: Datasheet (discontinued)

The N5 was the first warehouse drone from Nimbus Robotics. It was sold from 2020
to 2022 and then replaced by the N7. Support for the N5 ends in December 2025.

## Specifications
- Maximum flight time: 25 minutes per charge.
- Payload: 1.2 kg.
- Indoor cruising speed: 4 m/s.
- Maximum operating height: 9 m above the floor.
- Scanner: QR codes only, no RFID.
- Battery: lithium-polymer pack, rated for 300 charge cycles.
- Colour: white.

## Price and warranty
- List price at launch: 8,900 EUR.
- 12-month limited warranty. Nimbus Care+ was never offered for the N5.

## Firmware
- The N5 ran firmware with the codename "Stratus". The last release, Stratus 3.9,
  came out in March 2023. There will be no further updates.
