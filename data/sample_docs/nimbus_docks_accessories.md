# Nimbus Docks and Accessories

## D1 dock (for the N7)
- Charges an N7 from 15% to full in about 70 minutes.
- Draws up to 180 W while charging.
- The dock has its own 12-month warranty, separate from the drone's.
- Status LED: green means fully charged, amber means charging, and a blinking
  red light means a fault.

## Spare parts
- Rotor sets come in packs of four and should be replaced every 300 flight hours.
- Spare battery packs for the N7 can be ordered through the Halo portal.

## Site kit
- Every new site receives a kit with 10 high-visibility vests, 50 RFID test tags
  and a printed copy of the safety rules.
