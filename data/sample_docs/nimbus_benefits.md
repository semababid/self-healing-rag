# Nimbus Robotics — Employee Benefits & Ways of Working

This page complements the vacation policy in the Employee Handbook. All HR
requests go through the Halo portal unless stated otherwise.

## Working hours and remote work
- The standard full-time week is 38 hours.
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Field engineers and
  warehouse-lab staff agree remote days with their team lead.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00–15:00 local time in every office.

## Parental leave
- Nimbus tops up statutory parental pay to 100% of salary for the first
  12 weeks of leave, for parents of any gender.
- Employees returning from parental leave may work 80% hours at full pay for
  their first month back.

## Learning and development
- Each employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 EUR for courses,
  conferences, and books.
- Unused learning budget does not carry over into the next year.
- New engineers complete the Nimbus Pilot course in their first 60 days.

## Onboarding
- New hires receive their laptop and Halo portal access on day one.
- Every new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy from a different team for
  their first three months.
- Probation lasts six months.

## Health and wellbeing
- All employees can use a subsidised gym membership (Nimbus pays 50%).
- The Cottbus and Porto offices offer free lunch on Wednesdays; the Tallinn
  office provides a monthly meal allowance instead.

## Who to contact
- The People team is led by Amara Okafor, Head of People.
- Payroll questions go to payroll@nimbus.example.
