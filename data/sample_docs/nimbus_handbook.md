# Nimbus Robotics: Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

## Company facts
- Nimbus Robotics was founded in 2019 in Cottbus, Germany.
- The company builds warehouse inventory drones. Its flagship product is the **Nimbus N7**.
- The CEO is Dr. Lena Farkas. The CTO is Marco Reyes.
- As of 2024 the company employs 214 people across three offices: Cottbus, Porto, and Tallinn.

## The Nimbus N7 drone
- The N7 has a maximum flight time of 38 minutes on a single charge.
- It carries a 2.1 kg payload and cruises at 6.5 m/s indoors.
- The onboard scanner reads both QR codes and RFID tags.
- Firmware updates ship on the first Tuesday of every month under the codename "Cirrus".

## Vacation policy
- Full-time employees receive 30 paid vacation days per year.
- Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year; the rest expire on March 31.
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the Halo portal.
