# Nimbus N9: Product Preview

The N9 is the planned successor to the N7, aimed at large distribution centres.
It is scheduled to ship in the second half of 2025. All figures below are
targets from the prototype stage and may change before release.

## Planned specifications
- Flight time: 52 minutes per charge.
- Payload: 3.5 kg.
- Indoor cruising speed: 8 m/s.
- Maximum operating height: 14 m.
- Battery: lithium iron phosphate (LFP) pack, rated for 2,000 charge cycles.
- Colours: graphite grey or safety orange.
- Scanner: QR codes, RFID tags and a new long-range barcode camera.

## Pricing
- Expected list price: 21,500 EUR per drone.
- Customers who trade in N7 drones get 15% off.

## Compatibility
- The N9 uses a new dock, the D2. It cannot charge on N7 docks.
- N9 drones can share scan plans with N7 drones through fleet scheduling.
